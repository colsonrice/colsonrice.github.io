Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy covers the free own-key release of Liminal for Mac, provided by SquatchCraft LLC. Contact hello@squatchcraft.com with privacy questions.

Your manuscript stays in your local library

Liminal saves stories, chapters, plans, coaching results, preferences, and earlier versions on your Mac. This release does not provide Liminal account-based cloud sync. Backups and files you export are handled by the locations and services you choose.

When your words go to OpenAI

If you add an OpenAI API key and use online coaching, requests go directly from your Mac to OpenAI. Depending on the tool, a request may include your notes, plans, chapter text, story summaries, tasks, goals, and questions. The app describes what is sent beside requested tools and in Settings > Your words. Whole-story requests use summaries rather than sending the entire manuscript as raw text.

Online automatic readings can send the current chapter, tasks, and goals when you open a chapter, pause, or mark it complete. Automatic task gathering can send your plan. Review the mode and automatic-reading settings before use. Free mode sends nothing to OpenAI; supported readings set to This Mac run locally.

OpenAI receives request content, your API authentication, and ordinary connection information such as your IP address. OpenAI processes this data under its own terms and privacy practices, including applicable retention settings. See OpenAI's privacy policy and API data controls. SquatchCraft does not operate a coaching relay for the free own-key release and does not receive these coaching requests.

Your API key

Your key is stored in the macOS Keychain and used to authenticate your requests to OpenAI. It is not embedded in the app or sent to SquatchCraft. Remove it in Liminal Settings, or revoke it in your OpenAI account. Keychain entries can survive app removal.

Tracking, diagnostics, and support

Liminal has no advertising or analytics SDK. We do not sell your manuscript or personal information. Apple may handle diagnostics according to your system settings. If you contact support, we receive your email, message, and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send credentials or private manuscript content unless needed and deliberately chosen.

Your choices

You can use the writing tools without online coaching, remove your API key, export your stories, and move the local library to the Trash from Settings. Empty the Trash and remove separate backups or exports when you want those copies deleted too. Requests already sent to OpenAI are governed by its retention policies. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any required recordkeeping.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. Those providers receive ordinary connection information when you visit. These pages add no advertising or analytics scripts. See GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy.

Changes

We will update this policy when our practices change. Hosted coaching will require an updated disclosure before it becomes available.
