Can AI predict lottery numbers? We're finding out.

In theory, no: a fair draw is random. We are testing it anyway, in the open. Every line the app's Enhanced model makes is checked against the real draw, and the running count is right here, good nights and bad.

How to read those numbers

01 · NOT A PRIZE

One number pays nothing

Matching a single main number is not a prize in either game, and the gap has not shown up as prize money. This measures the picks, not winnings.

02 · NIGHTS, NOT LINES

Lines rise and fall together

Lines made for the same draw often share numbers, so one draw can lift or sink hundreds at once. A handful of very good nights carry much of the gap.

03 · STILL OPEN

It could be luck

There have not been enough draws to tell luck from skill. That is why this is an experiment and not a claim. The count keeps running either way.

Why the textbook answer is no

A lottery machine has no memory. The balls drawn last week have no effect on the balls drawn tonight, so a number that has not appeared for months is not "due". Powerball has 292,201,338 possible lines and Mega Millions 290,472,336, and in a fair draw each is as likely as any other.

That is the theory, and we think it deserves checking against real draws instead of being taken on trust in either direction. So the app keeps score, and we publish it.

How we keep it honest

We make PowerPlayAI, so we have every reason to oversell it. Instead we publish. Our house picks go up on a public page before each draw, with a timestamp anyone can check, and are scored against the real numbers afterwards. The community results show every model in the app side by side, including the free random one the Enhanced model is measured against.

If the gap closes, this page will show that too.

Signs a lottery "predictor" is not being straight with you

  • It promises prizes, or says it has cracked a pattern in the draws.
  • It charges for "predicted" numbers.
  • It shows its good nights and hides the rest.
  • It never says the draw is random.

Questions people ask

Is the Enhanced model ahead of random?

As of 12 November 2026, yes, on one measure: lines made by the Enhanced model have matched at least one main number more often than chance alone would give. The running count is at the top of this page. It may be luck, it is not a prize, and it says nothing about any future draw.

Does that mean an Enhanced line is more likely to pay out?

No. Matching one main number pays nothing, and the gap has not shown up as prize money. Every draw is random, and we make no claim about what any line will do next.

Can ChatGPT predict lottery numbers?

A chatbot will give you numbers, and nobody has shown that they do any differently from chance. It also keeps no record. The point of this experiment is the record: every line is checked against the real draw and counted, good nights and bad.

Do hot numbers come up more often?

They came up more often in the past, which is all "hot" means. Each draw is independent, so past frequency does not carry over to the next one. The app shows hot, cold and overdue numbers as history, not as a forecast.

How do I know the count is honest?

Our house picks for Powerball and Mega Millions are published before every draw with a timestamp anyone can check, and the community results are public, the losing nights included. Nothing is removed when a night goes badly.
