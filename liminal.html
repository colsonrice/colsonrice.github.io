Liminal · For fiction writers

Write your story. Keep your voice.

A writing space and fiction coach for Mac, from your first idea to your final manuscript.

Shape your notes into a plan you approve, write in a focused editor, and get coaching grounded in your own pages. Liminal helps you find your next step without writing your story for you.

Your writing comes first

  • Plan stories, organize chapters, and keep your notes beside your work.
  • Import existing drafts and export to Word, PDF, or plain text.
  • Find across your manuscript and restore earlier versions.
  • Ask for feedback and help when you are stuck, while keeping control of every sentence.

Free app. Your choice of coaching.

Writing, saving, and exporting are free. For OpenAI coaching, add your own API key in Settings. No Liminal subscription is required; OpenAI charges your account for usage. A ChatGPT subscription is not a substitute for API billing.

On compatible Macs, supported local readings can run without sending your words to OpenAI. Planning, feedback, and help when stuck require OpenAI. Automatic online readings can also use your key when enabled; review Settings > Your words before starting.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Local model features require compatible hardware and macOS 26 or later. Liminal is preparing for release; a download link will appear here when available. Hosted subscription plans are not available in this release.
