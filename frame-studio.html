Frame Studio · Art that feels at home

Art that feels at home

Museum paintings and your own photographs, prepared to fill your Samsung The Frame without an added border.

Search more than 5,400 paintings, save favorites, explore the history behind the art, and preview the crop before sending. Choose an optional color filter and adjust image darkening alongside TV brightness.

Make the wall your own

  • Landscape and portrait exports in 4K or Full HD.
  • Curations: handpicked paintings grouped by season, artist, and mood.
  • On-device photo cropping, filters, and image darkening.
  • Direct upload to compatible Frames, with JPEG export for SmartThings or USB as a fallback.

No account, ads, or subscription. Requires iOS 17 or later. Frame Studio is preparing for release; an App Store download link will appear here when available.
