How Mega Millions works

Main numbers
5 of 1–70
Mega Ball
1 of 1–24
Draw days
Tuesday · Friday
Possible lines
290,472,336

Mega Millions is played in the United States. A line is five different main numbers and one Mega Ball. That makes 290,472,336 possible lines, so any one line has a 1 in 290,472,336 chance of matching every number, however it was chosen.

What the app adds for Mega Millions

Four engines. Base learns from Mega Millions draw history. Random Balanced spreads a clean quick pick across the range. Neuron and Quantum come with the Enhanced subscription. All of them run on your phone.

The current rules. Since April 2025 the Mega Ball runs from 1 to 24, and both this page and the app use that range.

Published picks. Our own Mega Millions picks go up on the published picks page before every draw and are scored there afterwards.

A sealed result. After the draw, your result waits until you are ready, then opens ball by ball. You can turn the ceremony off and just see the numbers.

What "AI" means here, and what it does not

The engines in the app read past draws on your phone and shape a line from them. That changes which numbers you get. It does not change the odds. Every drawing is random and independent, and every possible line is exactly as likely as every other, whoever or whatever chose it.

So we do not ask you to take the picks on faith. Our house picks for Powerball and Mega Millions are published before each draw and scored after it, and the community results show how lines made in the app have done, on good nights and bad ones.

Questions people ask

How many numbers do I pick for Mega Millions?

Five main numbers from 1 to 70 and one Mega Ball from 1 to 24. The generator on this page follows those rules and never repeats a number within a line.

When is Mega Millions drawn?

Mega Millions is drawn every Tuesday and Friday. The PowerPlayAI app shows a countdown to the next draw and can remind you on draw night without giving away the result.

Does a number generator change my chances in Mega Millions?

No. There are 290,472,336 possible Mega Millions lines and each is equally likely in every drawing. A generator, an AI model and your own favourite numbers all have the same chance.

Can I check my Mega Millions ticket?

Yes, in the app: point the camera at the ticket or type the numbers in, and it compares each line with the draw.

Is this the official lottery site?

No. PowerPlayAI is an independent app and this page is not affiliated with Mega Millions or any lottery operator. Tickets are bought from official retailers.
